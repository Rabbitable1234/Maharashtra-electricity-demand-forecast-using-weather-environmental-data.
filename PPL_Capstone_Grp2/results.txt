================ CONCLUSION ================
1. Demand grew 45% over 2014-2023 (+16.8 MU/day each year), so trend is removed before relating it to weather.
2. After detrending, demand is highest in Summer (index 108.0) and lowest in Monsoon (94.5); seasonal differences are significant (ANOVA p=2.9e-291).
3. Strongest single weather link: t_max (r=+0.71). Weather + calendar explain R2=0.58 of daily demand variation; temperature terms add 0.11.
4. Weekdays draw about 2.4 index points more than weekends (likely economic-activity effect).
5. SARIMA(2, 1, 2)x(0,1,1,12) hold-out MAE=25.7 MU/day (4.5% MAPE) vs seasonal-naive 48.9 -> beats the baseline.
6. Limitations: state-average weather, no holiday/industrial data, 2020 COVID disruption, gaps in reporting.
